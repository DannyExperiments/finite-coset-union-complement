# Contributing

Contributions are welcome in four narrowly separated lanes:

1. **proof audit** — identify a precise inference to verify or challenge;
2. **literature audit** — provide a directly relevant source with exact
   hypotheses and theorem location;
3. **formalization** — extend the Lean certificate without changing the
   mathematical statement;
4. **reproducibility** — repair build, checksum, or disclosure infrastructure.

Every proposed change should identify the affected claim, whether validity,
scope, priority, presentation, or formal verification changes, and the exact
commands and evidence used.

Do not represent computation as a universal proof; broaden the Lean-verified
scope without a kernel-checked declaration; claim human peer review or
historical priority; add private receipts, chat URLs, local paths, or
third-party source PDFs; or overwrite frozen release assets.
