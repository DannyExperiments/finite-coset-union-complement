# Literature and priority audit summary

Search cutoff: 2026-08-02

The audit verified the wording and attribution of Kourovka Notebook Problem
21.115 and inspected the official 2026 versions through arXiv v45, dated
3 July 2026. The problem remained unannotated there.

The earliest identical conjectural formulation located was Benjamin Sambale
and Marius Tărnăuceanu,
[*On the size of coset unions*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10801-021-01079-x),
published online in 2021 and in *Journal of Algebraic Combinatorics* 55
(2022), 979–987. That paper proved weaker universal bounds and exact special
cases but not the full arbitrary-finite-group result.

The search found prior ingredients related to Hadamard-rank inequalities,
support-rank uncertainty principles, group-algebra methods, minrank, and
polynomial covering. It did not locate the exact coset-label matrix
construction, an identical proof, or a stronger theorem implying the stated
result.
